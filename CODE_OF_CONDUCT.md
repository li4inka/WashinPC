# Community conduct

Be respectful, discuss the work rather than the person, and make room for different experience levels. Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, and disclosure of another person's private information are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove abusive content and restrict participation. Report a concern through a minimal [issue](https://github.com/li4inka/WashinPC/issues/new), without repeating private or harmful material; ask the maintainer to arrange a private conversation when necessary.
